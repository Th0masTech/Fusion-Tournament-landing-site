Rules

Welcome to the Fusion Tournament! These are the rules:

Rules may change on tournament day. Any changes will be announced before matches start, and organizers have the final say.

Super Smash Bros:

  • Ruleset: 3 Stock
  • Time Limit: 6–8 minutes
  • Items: Off
  • Stage Selection: Anyone
  • Stage Hazard Toggle: Off
  • Final Smash Meter: Off
  • Spirits: Off
  • Damage Handicap: Off
  • First to: 1 Win
  • Launch Rate: 1.0x
  • Underdog Boost: Off
  • Score Display: Off
  • % Show Damage: Yes
  • Custom Balance: Off
  • Echo Fighters: Separate
  • Radar: Big
  • Mii Fighters and their respective custom moves are allowed. Movesets must be shown in the name (e.g., 1111, 2223, 3121).
  • If time runs out: winner is determined first by stocks, then percentage.
  • If both stock and percentage are equal, or both players lose their last stock at the same time: a tiebreaker is played (1 stock, 3 minutes, same characters and stage).
  • Pausing is turned off.

FC 27:

  • Ruleset: 1v1
  • Time Limit: 12 minutes
  • Half Time: 6 minutes
  • Overtime: 2 minutes
  • Stadium: Any
  • VAR: On
  • Custom Tactics: Allowed
  • Player Switching: Allowed
  • Pausing: Off
